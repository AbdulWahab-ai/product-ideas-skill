How to read the price. "Minimum sale price" is the lowest price at which the product is still worth selling: the entry plan or smallest deal, estimated from comparable products. It is a starting point for your own pricing, not a quote. Fit rates each idea for . Ideas with a blue edge are the strongest fits.

How the score works. Each idea is rated 1–5 on eight criteria, multiplied by the weight, and scaled to 100. Open Decision details on any card for market size, unit economics, team, first-version cost, hardware, legal requirements, platform risk, a kill criterion and the full working.

Judgement inputs: buyer counts, the share likely to buy, typical deal, team size, delivery hours and customer targets are estimates from public market figures and comparable products, not measured data. The share likely to buy is the most uncertain input, so test it first: the market size moves in direct proportion to it. Seven of the eight score ratings (all except deal size) are judgements against the rubric above. Check buyer counts in a prospect database before relying on them.
